### Overall Impression
The interface has a solid structural foundation (2/3 tasks, 1/3 stats split) and smart workflow features, but it's held back by high visual noise, tedious modal interactions, and heavy reliance on AI-generated aesthetic clichés (badges, emojis, mono-eyebrows).

### What's Working
1. **The "Skip for now" mechanic**: A smart workflow feature that respects the user's immediate context without breaking data integrity.
2. **Split-pane layout**: The toggleable stats pane keeps the primary task focus clean and adaptable.
3. **Inline Quick Note**: Reduces modal fatigue for simple data entry.

### Priority Issues

1. **[P1] What**: Tedious modal dropdowns (Log Call).
   **Why it matters**: A 9-item dropdown is an RSI risk and a cognitive speedbump for someone doing 100+ calls a day.
   **Fix**: Expose the options as a grid of buttons or grouped radio choices for immediate recognition.
   **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`

2. **[P1] What**: "Badge Soup" overload.
   **Why it matters**: Task rows display up to 5 colored badges at once, creating visual noise that makes it hard to scan priority.
   **Fix**: Consolidate status, priority, and channel into a cleaner visual language rather than stacking multiple tinted pills.
   **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill`

3. **[P2] What**: Typographic density (Mono eyebrows).
   **Why it matters**: Ubiquitous `text-xs font-mono uppercase` labels make the interface feel cramped and artificially scaled.
   **Fix**: Rely on font weight and proximity instead of tracking and all-caps to denote hierarchy.
   **Suggested command**: `$impeccable typeset`

4. **[P3] What**: Overuse of native emojis.
   **Why it matters**: Default emojis (`🎉`, `📞`) cheapen the impact and look amateurish when a cohesive Lucide icon set is already available.
   **Fix**: Replace native emojis with Lucide icons to elevate the premium feel.
   **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish`
